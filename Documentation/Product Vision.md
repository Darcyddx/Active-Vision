## 🌟 Product Vision

### What Are We Building?
Active Vision is an innovative tennis performance analysis Android app powered by advanced computer vision and machine learning. We’re creating a system that captures and dissects tennis swing mechanics in real-time using high-speed cameras and intelligent algorithms, delivering actionable insights through an intuitive, user-friendly interface.

### Who Is It For?
This product is designed for **tennis players** of all levels—from aspiring amateurs to competitive athletes—and their **coaches**. Whether you’re a beginner looking to master the basics or a seasoned player aiming to fine-tune your game, Active Vision is your ultimate training companion. Tennis academies and sports technology enthusiasts may also find value in integrating this system into their programs.

### Why Will They Use or Buy It?
Players and coaches will turn to Active Vision because it transforms how they approach training. Unlike traditional methods that rely on subjective observation or post-match video reviews, our system offers **real-time, data-driven feedback** that pinpoints strengths and weaknesses with precision. They’ll use it to:
- **Boost Performance:** Gain detailed insights into swing speed, angle, and impact accuracy to optimize every shot.
- **Accelerate Improvement:** Receive instant suggestions to adjust techniques during practice, saving time and enhancing learning.
- **Track Progress:** Monitor development over time with personalized data and reports, fostering motivation and accountability.

Active Vision empowers users to train smarter, not just harder, making it an highly efficient tool for anyone who want to elevate their tennis games.
